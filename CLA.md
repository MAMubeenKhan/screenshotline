This exists for one reason, and it is worth stating plainly rather than burying:
Screenshotline is AGPL-3.0, and a hosted commercial version is offered alongside
it. Without an agreement like this, contributed code could only ever be used
under the AGPL — which would make it impossible to offer the hosted service, or
to relicense later, without tracking down every contributor. Retrofitting this
after the fact is effectively impossible, which is why it is here from the
start.

If you would rather not sign, that is entirely reasonable. Open an issue instead
— a good bug report with a reproduction is often worth more than a patch, and
carries no paperwork.

## How to sign

Sign automatically when you open a pull request — a bot will comment with a link
the first time, and you will not be asked again.

For a paper trail, or if the bot is unavailable, comment on your pull request
with:

> I have read the CLA and I hereby sign the CLA.
